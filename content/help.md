## How do I get access?

Email [hello@stokeranker.com](mailto:hello@stokeranker.com?subject=StokeRanker%20access%20request) and say whether you’d like the **TestFlight trial** for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch, a **link to the web app**, or both. Rob will reply with the invitation or link. No account or sign-up is needed.

## Is it free?

Yes. It costs $0, with no subscription, no ads, and no paid tier. StokeRanker is an independent project, built to surf this stretch of coast better.

## Which devices does it support?

The native app is built for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Recording surfs needs an Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later, paired with your iPhone. Install Apple’s TestFlight app to accept a beta invitation. The web app works in a modern browser, for forecasts only.

## Where does it cover?

Seventeen breaks on the Coffs Coast, from Sawtell to Arrawarra: Sawtell Southies, Sawtell Main, Sawtell Island, Sawtell Trapdoors, Gallows, North Wall, Park Beach, Macauleys, Emerald Beach, Shellys, Sandy Beach, Serenity, Woolgoolga Beach, Woolgoolga Back Beach, Mullawarra, Mullaway, and Arrawarra. More of the coast will be added over time. Each break is modelled on its own, rather than one forecast for the whole coastline.

## What do the scores mean?

Each break gets a score out of 100 for each session, with a plain-word summary from *unsurfable* to *good*. The score depends on the surfer profile you choose (All-round, Mellow, or Performance), so a mellow morning and a punchy performance session can rank the breaks differently. Open a break to see the swell, wind, tide, and shelter behind its score. A higher score is an estimate, not a promise of good or safe surf.

## Why are there different models and forecast sources?

The forecast source (ECMWF or GFS) supplies the wind and waves. The scoring model decides what those conditions mean at each break, so you can pick the one that suits how you surf:

- **Everyday** scores the typical wave at each break. It suits surfers who want a wave every few minutes, and it is the fixed reference the others are measured against.
- **Sets** lets the sets decide on small days, less a charge for the wait between them. It suits surfers happy to sit and wait for a set.
- **Live-Buoy Sets** is Sets with today’s swell corrected to the live Coffs Harbour wave buoy. It helps most on southerly swell days at sheltered breaks, and only changes the first day of the forecast.

You can switch the model and the forecast source separately. The models keep improving: rated surfs logged on the beach are checked against every model, the models are refitted as the checks build up, and new ones are added as they’re tested. Telling Rob which combination matched the beach is the most useful thing you can do in the trial.

## How do I record a surf?

Open StokeRanker on your Apple Watch and start a surf before you paddle out. Water Lock comes on, so there’s nothing to touch while you surf. Finish when you’re back on the beach, and the surf goes to your iPhone’s **Surfs** tab. Each surf shows a satellite map of your rides, with every ride counted, measured, and timed. StokeRanker suggests the break from where your rides started; you can confirm it, or name your own spot.

Rate the waves first and the app then shows what the forecast said for the time you were out, so the forecast doesn’t sway your answer. The **Scoreboard** adds up your surfs for the week, month, year, and all time, with your bests and favourite breaks.

## Can I report a forecast that looks wrong?

Yes, please. Use the feedback button on a forecast, or email Rob with the break, date, time, and what you saw. Spot checks with photos are invite-only, for a small group of regular observers on the web app. Ask Rob if you’d like to help.

## Can I rely on it for safety?

No. It’s a forecast, not a lifeguard. It can’t see the rip in front of you, the rocks showing at low tide, or how far out of your depth you’d be. If you’re leaving it to an app to decide whether to paddle out, leave the board in the car.

Look at the water, check the official warnings, and know your limits. If in doubt, don’t go out.

## What happens to my data?

Feedback and spot checks are optional. The [privacy policy](/privacy/) explains what is stored on your device, what reaches the service, and which copies you can delete.

## How do I delete all my stored data?

There is no single “delete everything” button. Deleting a surf in the app hides it but keeps its track and other data. Your private journal is held on your devices and in your iCloud, which we cannot access or delete for you. Shared ratings are deidentified: we do not know which ratings belong to you, so we cannot identify them for deletion in response to a request.

1. **Stop sharing ratings.** Turn off **Settings → Surf journal → Share ratings** on each device to stop new submissions. This does not erase ratings already sent. If you want to withdraw individual ratings first, clear them while sharing is still enabled and allow the app to connect: it requests removal using the identifiers stored on that device.
2. **Remove the device copies.** Delete StokeRanker from every iPhone and iPad that holds your journal, and remove the Watch app too. Choose **Delete App**, rather than offloading it or just removing its Home Screen icon. Do this before deleting the iCloud copy, so a device with an old journal cannot upload it again. The rating-sharing identifier is stored separately in the device’s Keychain and may survive uninstalling; removing the app does not erase the matching server record.
3. **Remove the iCloud copy.** On your iPhone or iPad, open **Settings → your name → iCloud → Storage** (or **Manage Account Storage**), select **StokeRanker**, and use the option to delete its data. This removes the private cloud journal, including hidden surfs. Turning sync off alone does not erase it. If StokeRanker is not listed or no deletion option appears, contact us for help; we cannot delete your private iCloud data on your behalf. Apple explains [how third-party app data is stored in iCloud](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/icloud/mm62d92d6b3e/icloud).
4. **Remove Health data separately.** In the Health app, review StokeRanker under your profile’s **Apps** or **Apps and Services**, and delete the data it saved. Deleting StokeRanker or its iCloud journal does not delete Health workouts or routes. Keep data from other apps unless you also want to remove it.
5. **Check backups, files, and shared copies.** Remove any exported cards or original import files you no longer want, including copies in Files, Photos, or other services and their Recently Deleted folders. Review device backups separately: older backups may contain app data, and restoring one can bring it back. Apple’s [iCloud storage guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108922) explains backup management. Avoid deleting a whole-device backup unless you intend to lose the other data in it too. Copies sent to other people or services must be removed there.
6. **Clear the web app’s local data.** Clear the StokeRanker web app’s cookies and website data in your browser. This removes local preferences and the browser’s device identifier; it does not delete server records.

These steps remove the copies you control; they do not erase previously shared ratings from our server. Server retention and backups are described in the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
