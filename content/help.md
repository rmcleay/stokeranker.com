## How do I get access?

Email [hello@stokeranker.com](mailto:hello@stokeranker.com?subject=StokeRanker%20access%20request) and say whether you’d like the **TestFlight trial**, a **link to the web app**, or both. Rob will reply with the invitation or link. There is no public signup form.

## Which devices does it support?

The native app is built for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Install Apple’s TestFlight app to accept a beta invitation. The web app works in a modern browser.

## Where does it cover?

StokeRanker currently focuses on the Coffs Coast, from Arrawarra to Emerald. It is built around individual local breaks rather than a forecast for the whole coastline.

## What do the scores mean?

Scores compare predicted surf suitability for the selected surfer profile, session and scoring model. Open a break to inspect the forecast and its reasons. A higher score is a modelling estimate, not a guarantee of good or safe surf.

## Why are there different models and forecast sources?

The scoring model controls how conditions are assessed at a break. The forecast source controls the weather and wave inputs. You can compare them independently. Experimental options are trials, not a promise of greater accuracy.

## Can I report a forecast that looks wrong?

Yes. Use the feedback form where available, or email Rob with the break, date, time and what you saw. Invited web users can also submit spot checks and optional photos. Native spot-check submission is not yet available; the app links to the website.

## Can I rely on it for safety?

Use it to help plan, alongside your own assessment and current official warnings. Conditions change, and the forecast cannot assess your ability, rips, hazards or whether a particular paddle-out is safe.

## What happens to my data?

Feedback and spot checks are optional. The [privacy policy](/privacy/) explains what is stored on your device, what reaches the service and how to request deletion.

## Contact

For bugs, access, questions or suggestions: [hello@stokeranker.com](mailto:hello@stokeranker.com). Include your device, app version if known, and what you were doing when something went wrong. Please avoid sending private invite links.
