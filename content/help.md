## How do I get access?

Email [hello@stokeranker.com](mailto:hello@stokeranker.com?subject=StokeRanker%20access%20request) and say whether you’d like the **TestFlight trial**, a **link to the web app**, or both. Rob will reply with the invitation or link. There is no public signup form.

## Is it free?

Yes. Free for 100 years. No ads, no subscription, no paid tier. StokeRanker is an independent project, built to surf this stretch of coast better, and you don’t pay to help test it.

## Which devices does it support?

The native app is built for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Install Apple’s TestFlight app to accept a beta invitation. The web app works in a modern browser.

## Where does it cover?

Eight breaks on the Coffs Coast’s northern beaches: Arrawarra, Mullawarra, Woolgoolga Back Beach, Woolgoolga Beach, Serenity, Sandy Beach, Shellys and Emerald Beach. That’s where it starts, because every forecast there can be checked against the surf. Coffs Harbour’s own beaches and more of the coast will be added as the model improves. Each break is modelled on its own, rather than one forecast for the whole coastline.

## What do the scores mean?

Each break gets a score out of 100 for each session, with a plain-word summary from *unsurfable* to *good*. The score depends on the surfer profile you choose (All-round, Mellow or Performance), so a mellow morning and a punchy performance session can rank the breaks differently. Open a break to see the swell, wind, tide and shelter behind its score. A higher score is an estimate, not a promise of good or safe surf.

## Why are there different models and forecast sources?

The forecast source (ECMWF or GFS) supplies the wind and waves. The scoring model (Original or Experimental) decides what those conditions mean at each break. You can switch either one on its own. Experimental models are trials, not a promise of greater accuracy. Telling Rob which combination matched the beach is the most useful thing you can do in the trial.

## Can I report a forecast that looks wrong?

Yes, please. Use the feedback button on a forecast, or email Rob with the break, date, time and what you saw. Spot checks with photos are open to a small group of regular observers on the web app. Ask Rob if you’d like to help.

## Can I rely on it for safety?

Use it to help plan, alongside your own assessment and current official warnings. Conditions change, and the forecast cannot assess your ability, rips, hazards or whether a particular paddle-out is safe.

## What happens to my data?

Feedback and spot checks are optional. The [privacy policy](/privacy/) explains what is stored on your device, what reaches the service and how to request deletion.

## Contact

For bugs, access, questions or suggestions: [hello@stokeranker.com](mailto:hello@stokeranker.com). Include your device, app version if known, and what you were doing when something went wrong. If Rob sent you a personal web link, please don’t share it.
