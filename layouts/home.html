Same swell. Different beach.

PICK YOUR MODEL

Score it the way you surf.

The same forecast can be read more than one way. Choose the scoring model that matches how you like to surf, and switch whenever you want a second opinion. You can also switch the forecast itself, between ECMWF and GFS.

The models keep getting better. Every surf rated on the beach is checked against what each model said, and the models are refitted as the checks build up. New models join the list as they’re tested, and the forecast starts from whichever one has matched the beach best.

  1. Everyday

    Scores the typical wave at each break. For surfers who want a wave every few minutes. The fixed reference, built by hand from local knowledge.

  2. Sets

    On small days the sets decide the score, less a charge for the wait between them. For surfers happy to sit and wait for a set.

  3. Live-Buoy Sets

    Sets, with today’s swell corrected to the live Coffs Harbour wave buoy. Best on southerly swell days at sheltered breaks.

BUILT HERE. STILL LEARNING.

A forecast you can question.

StokeRanker combines public marine and weather forecasts with local break knowledge, tide modelling, seabed data, and headland shelter.

It now covers 17 breaks from Sawtell to Arrawarra, each modelled on its own, and more of the coast will follow.

It’s an independent project by Rob McLeay, checked since October 2026 against rated sessions logged on the beach. Scores are estimates, and the models are still developing. Your eyes on the beach matter.

  1. Choose your time.

  2. Compare the coast.

    Look at the rankings, wave size, wind, and the explanation behind each score.

  3. Tell Rob which forecast was right.

    Use the feedback button on a forecast, or email the break, time, and what you saw.
