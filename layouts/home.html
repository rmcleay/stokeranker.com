A LITTLE LOCAL KNOWLEDGE

More than an offshore swell number.

A headland, the tide and the angle of the swell can change the story. StokeRanker models those differences for each break.

01 / COMPARE

BUILT HERE. STILL LEARNING.

A forecast you can question.

StokeRanker combines public marine and weather forecasts with local break knowledge, tide modelling, seabed data and headland shelter.

It’s an independent project by Rob McLeay, being tested against real surf observations. Scores are estimates, and the models are still developing. Your eyes on the beach matter.

  1. Choose your time.

    Start with the session you can actually surf.

  2. Compare the coast.

    Look at the rankings, wave size, wind and the explanation behind each score.

  3. Tell Rob what you found.

    Optional forecast feedback and web spot checks help test the predictions against the real thing.
