BUILT HERE. STILL LEARNING.

A forecast you can question.

StokeRanker combines public marine and weather forecasts with local break knowledge, tide modelling, seabed data and headland shelter.

It starts with eight breaks on the northern beaches, where every forecast can be checked against the surf. More of the coast will follow as the model improves.

It’s an independent project by Rob McLeay, checked since October 2026 against rated sessions logged on the beach. Scores are estimates, and the models are still developing. Your eyes on the beach matter.
